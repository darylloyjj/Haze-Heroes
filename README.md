# Haze-Heroes

A 2D pixel-art roguelike set in Singapore during the haze.

The haze has come alive. Play as Jun Jie, wake up in hospital, grab a mask and a weapon, and fight your way through Singapore to find your family.

**Play it here:** https://yourname.github.io/haze-heroes/

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / arrow keys | Joystick |
| Attack | J / left click | ATTACK |
| Special | K / right click | SPECIAL |
| Chao Keng (dash) | Space | CHAO KENG |
| Interact | E / Enter | E |
| Pause | P / Esc | PAUSE |

## Features
- Two weapons: the Chinese Fan and the Cyclone Vac
- Power-ups from the PE Teacher, Hawker Auntie and Sim Lim Uncle
- Physio upgrades bought with Clean Air
- The mama shop, breakable boxes and three random room types
- A higher PSI means more Hazed and bigger rewards

Progress saves in your browser.
